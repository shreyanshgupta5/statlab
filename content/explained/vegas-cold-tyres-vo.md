# Voiceover script: "Why does Vegas eat cold tyres?" (46 s)

Read at a relaxed, confident pace (about 2.5 words a second). Each block fits its time slot; start each line as the new headline appears.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 – 4.6 | WHY DOES VEGAS EAT COLD TYRES? (neon track) | "Las Vegas is the one race where F1 tyres just won't wake up. Here's why." |
| 4.6 – 11.2 | TYRES NEED HEAT (gauge swings to 100°C) | "F1 tyres only grip properly in a narrow window, around a hundred degrees. Below that, the rubber is hard and slippery." |
| 11.2 – 18.8 | COLDEST TRACK OF 2025 (24-race bar chart) | "And Vegas is the coldest track of the year. Eighteen degrees on race night in 2025. Mexico was fifty-one." |
| 18.8 – 26.4 | STRAIGHTS COOL THEM DOWN (lap map, 69% flat out) | "The lap makes it worse. Almost seventy percent is flat out, on long straights in cold night air, with few big corners to put heat back in." |
| 26.4 – 33.6 | COLD TYRES SLIDE (tread, graining) | "So the tyres slide instead of gripping, and the sliding tears the surface into little rolls of rubber. That's graining." |
| 33.6 – 40.6 | SO TEAMS GO HARD (81% / 77% / 61%) | "That's why teams lean on the hard tyre. And last year, nine of the top ten stopped just once." |
| 40.6 – 46.0 | WARM TYRES WIN VEGAS (follow) | "Whoever switches their tyres on first wins Vegas. Who's your pick? Follow for more." |

## Facts behind each line

- Track temperatures: OpenF1 weather data, median over each 2025 race. Vegas 17.7°C (coldest of 24), Mexico City 51.3°C (hottest), season median about 34°C.
- 69% full throttle: Russell's 2024 Las Vegas pole lap, OpenF1 car data.
- Hard-tyre share (81 / 77 / 61%) and stops (2023 and 2024 mostly two stops; 2025: 9 of the top 10 one-stopped): OpenF1 stints for the top 10 finishers.
- "Around 100°C" working window and graining from sliding: general tyre behaviour, said as an approximation. The tyre-temperature bar on the lap map is labelled as an illustration (teams don't publish tyre temperatures).
- Race time on the end card: Saturday 21 November, 8 pm local (04:00 UTC Sunday).
