## Facts behind each line

- Coldest track of 2025: median race track temperature from OpenF1 weather for all 24 Grands Prix. Vegas 17.7°C, Mexico City 51.3°C, season median about 34°C.
- Cooling through the race: OpenF1 race weather, plotted from minute 4. In 2025 the first readings around the start spike to 24–28°C (most likely grid heat), so they're left out. Start → finish: 2023 18.5 → 17.6°C, 2024 18.0 → 16.6°C, 2025 18.2 → 16.7°C.
- 69% full throttle, 14% braking, 1.8 km flat-out run: Russell's 2024 Las Vegas pole lap (OpenF1 car data).
- Stints: OpenF1 stints for the top 10 finishers, 2023–25. 2025: nine of the ten made one stop (Russell's data shows a second, one-lap stint).
- "Roughly 100°C" window, heat sources and graining: general tyre behaviour, shown as illustrations.
- 2026 standings after round 20: ONE F1 app data. Race start: Saturday 21 November, 8 pm local (04:00 UTC Sunday).
